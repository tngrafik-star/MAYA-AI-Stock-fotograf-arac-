Stock Photo & Etsy Metadata Limits 2026: Adobe Stock, Shutterstock and Etsy Rules

📅 October 23, 2026 ⏱️ 6 min read ✍️ MayaListing Team

Every image you upload to a stock site or Etsy needs a title, description and keywords that fit the platform's rules. Keywords past the limit are silently cut, tags that break the rules are rejected, and metadata in the wrong language never shows up in search. Here are the limits of the three biggest platforms in one table.

The Limits at a Glance

PlatformTitleKeywords / tagsLanguage and characters
Adobe StockUp to 200 characters; under 70 recommendedUp to 49; the first 10 carry the most weightEnglish
ShutterstockThe description doubles as the title, up to 200 charactersBetween 7 and 50English
EtsyUp to 140 charactersExactly 13 tags, each up to 20 charactersLetters, numbers, spaces and - ' ™ © ®

Adobe Stock

Adobe Stock shortens titles in search results, so fit the key information into the first 70 characters. Keywords are capped at 49 and order matters: the first 10 weigh the most in search. Put the main subject, the concept and the use case in those first 10.

Shutterstock

Shutterstock has no separate title field; your description is used as the title. Write one sentence of up to 200 characters that describes the image. Use between 7 and 50 keywords: fewer than 7 blocks the upload, and more than 50 are not accepted.

Etsy

Etsy listing titles are limited to 140 characters. Tags follow a strict rule: 13 tags, each up to 20 characters. Tags may only contain letters, numbers, spaces and - ' ™ © ®; symbols like & or / are rejected. Use all 13 and prefer short phrases buyers actually search for (e.g. "ceramic mug", "gift mug") over single words.

The 5 Most Common Mistakes

  • Non-English metadata on stock sites. Buyers on Adobe Stock and Shutterstock search in English; other languages make your image nearly invisible.
  • Repeated keywords. "coffee" and "Coffee" are the same keyword; repeats only waste your allowance.
  • Lists over the limit. Adobe Stock cuts everything after 49. If your key term is at the end, it is gone.
  • Irrelevant keyword stuffing. Listing things that are not in the image is a common reason for rejection.
  • Etsy tags over 20 characters. Long tags are not saved; shorten the phrase or split it in two.
