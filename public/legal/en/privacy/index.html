Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

1. Data Collected

When using our Platform, your name, email address and password are collected for membership operations; payment and billing information processed by the Lemon Squeezy payment platform are collected for payment operations. Images you upload for analysis are sent to Google Gemini for AI analysis; a downscaled copy is kept in a private store only you can access, to show in your analysis history; you can permanently delete your account, history and images at any time with "Delete Account" on the Account page.

2. Processing and Security of Images

The photos you upload are processed solely for generating metadata specific to you. Your images are never shared with third parties, sold, or used as datasets for general training of our AI models. Your files are transferred to our servers using secure encryption methods (SSL/TLS).

3. Cookies

Essential cookies and browser storage keep you signed in and remember your language and theme. Google Analytics and Google Ads cookies are enabled only if you accept them in the cookie notice; you can change your choice at any time with the "Cookie Preferences" link at the bottom of the page.

4. Third-Party Services

MayaListing uses Supabase (database and user management), Lemon Squeezy (payment integration), Google Gemini (AI model) and, with your consent, Google Analytics/Google Ads (measurement and advertising) services for infrastructure. The privacy and security policies of these platforms apply.

5. Contact

For all questions and requests regarding our privacy policy, you can contact us at destek.mayalisting@gmail.com.
